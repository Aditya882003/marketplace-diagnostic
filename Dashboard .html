Executive diagnostic

Growth is acquisition-led; delivery timing is the strongest review signal.

In the verified window, 96.4% of 2018 orders came from first-time buyers while the censoring-adjusted fixed-90-day repeat rate is 2.4%. Delivery timing explains 17.4% of review-score variance in a one-way six-band decomposition, versus 0.8% for primary category and 0.8% for customer state.

Finding one · growth & retention

The marketplace remains acquisition-heavy.

Only 2.4% of eligible customers place another order within 90 days. Cohorts remain low rather than showing a clear deterioration, and repeat rates by first-order score are close together.

Implication

The evidence supports an acquisition-heavy growth model. It does not support a strong claim that review scores alone explain low repeat.

Delivery remains the clearest operational association to test. A holdout-based experiment can distinguish improvement from correlation.

Finding two · logistics & CX

Late delivery coincides with a steep review penalty.

On-time delivered reviewed orders average 4.28 stars; late delivered reviewed orders average 2.55. The decline becomes especially pronounced after three days late.

Finding three · concentration & region

The regional gap is a transport proxy, not a standalone review driver.

Customer state accounts for only 0.8% of one-way review-score variance. Yet Northeast customer states show higher late rates than São Paulo, consistent with longer seller-to-customer lanes. The stronger review signal is lateness itself.

Data & Assumptions · verified against attached CSVs

Cleaning steps

  • Reviews reduced to one per order (latest kept).
  • Customers resolved on customer_unique_id.
  • 775 orders with no items kept with GMV = 0.
  • 1 order with no payment row.
  • 610 products with missing category labelled Unknown.
  • 2 untranslated category labels retained in Portuguese.
  • Analysis window closed 31 Aug 2018 because Sep–Oct 2018 hold only 20 orders.
  • Primary order category / seller uses the first listed line item.

Definitions

  • GMV = item price + freight.
  • On-time = delivered on or before the estimated date.
  • Seller analysis uses sellers with 20+ delivered reviewed orders.
  • Repeat rate is censoring-adjusted to a fixed 90-day window.
  • “1-star” = review_score exactly 1. Any 1–2-star metric is labelled explicitly.
  • Variance chart is one-way eta²; overlapping drivers are not additive.
